About Me

I take a lot of pictures; things that catch my eye, small moments I want to remember, and cute birds I see on the sidewalk. Most of the photos don't turn out very good, but the few that do will be posted here. This is primarily a collection of my 35mm film shots, but I also post some bonus ambient motion excerpts.
